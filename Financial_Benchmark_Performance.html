    Sources on file

    Reported vs. derived values

    Revenue, growth %, gross margin %, operating margin %, net margin % and EBT are reported figures from the sources above. Gross Profit, OPEX (absolute), EBIT and Net Profit (absolute) are derived: computed from the reported percentages against reported revenue, not separately re-verified line items.

    Formula reference

    YoY growth = (current − prior) / |prior| × 100
    CAGR (5yr) = (last / first)^(1/years) − 1
    DSO = Receivables / Revenue × 365
    DIO = Inventory / COGS-proxy × 365
    DPO = Payables / COGS-proxy × 365
    CCC = DSO + DIO − DPO
    Free cash flow = Operating cash flow + Capex (capex reported negative)
